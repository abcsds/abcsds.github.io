The HeartRateLab is a new, powerful, and complete computational framework, written in Julia, for the processing, analysis, modeling, and evaluation of human heart Inter-Beat-Intervals (IBIs). Heart rate time signals are one of the most readily available and cost effective biosignals for the study of human behavior, and their availability and ease of use make them ideal for open science. However, advanced functionality has so far been provided mostly by private software, while many open source libraries are designed as simple feature extraction tools and have fallen into disuse.

Leveraging Julia's scientific ecosystem — including DifferentialEquations.jl and Turing.jl — HeartRateLab provides comprehensive IBI processing, complete feature extraction from entire recordings or with windowed approaches, and complex system modeling with parameter recovery. The package is registered in the Julia General registry and is documented and publicly installable.
